\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:preliminaries}

An affine map transforms an input vector into an output vector using a linear
transformation and an additive offset:
\begin{equation}
    \y = \A\x + \b.
    \label{eq:affine-map}
\end{equation}
\begin{notationtable}
    $\x$ & $\reals^n$ & Input vector
        & Vector supplied to the affine map. \\
    $\y$ & $\reals^m$ & Output vector
        & Result of the affine map. \\
    $\A$ & $\reals^{m\times n}$ & Linear transformation
        & Matrix mapping the input to the output space. \\
    $\b$ & $\reals^m$ & Additive offset
        & Vector added to the transformed input. \\
    $m$ & $\naturals$ & Output dimension
        & Positive integer giving the number of output components. \\
    $n$ & $\naturals$ & Input dimension
        & Positive integer giving the number of input components. \\
\end{notationtable}
